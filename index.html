OUR STORY

The pulse of Motbung.

Orion Pulse Technologies was born from a simple belief: world-class technology does not have to come from the world's biggest tech hubs.

It can be built wherever there is talent, resilience, ambition, and the courage to start.

01 / ROOTED HERE

From Motbung, Manipur to digital frontiers.

Founded in Motbung, along the foothills of Manipur, Orion Pulse began with a group of local technology enthusiasts asking a simple question:

“Why can't we build the future right here?”

That question became our foundation. We wanted to create a technology company that could serve clients beyond our borders while creating meaningful opportunities for talented people within our own community.

02

Local roots.
Global ambition.

Our development operations are rooted in Manipur, while our work reaches businesses and ideas far beyond it.

03

Technology with

04

Keeping our feet in Motbung, while our eyes stay on the horizon.
